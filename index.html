Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use this oil?

Apply the oil to your scalp and hair, massage gently with your fingertips, leave it on for 1-2 hours or overnight, then wash with a mild shampoo.

How often should I use it?

For best results, use it two to three times a week as part of your regular hair care routine.

Is it suitable for all hair types?

Yes. The blend is gentle enough for men, women and every hair type, including dry and damaged hair.

How long should I leave it on?

Leave it on for at least 1-2 hours, or overnight for deeper nourishment.

How should I store it?

Store the bottle in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, and keep the cap tightly closed.

How can I order?

Add the oil to your cart and check out online, or message us on WhatsApp to place your order.
